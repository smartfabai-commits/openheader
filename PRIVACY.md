# Privacy Policy — OpenHeader

_Last updated: 2026-07-24_

OpenHeader does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or browsing activity.

- OpenHeader does **not** read the content of your web traffic.
- Header modifications are applied via Chrome's official `declarativeNetRequest` API. The extension does **not** intercept or inspect network requests.
- Your header configurations are stored **locally** in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`). They are never sent to us or any third party.
- We use **no** analytics, **no** trackers, and **no** advertising SDKs.

If the optional Pro sync feature is enabled in the future, only the header profile data you explicitly choose to sync will be stored, solely to provide the sync service. This will be clearly opt-in.
